Send DTS to a receiver (experimental)

Sends DTS to a receiver or soundbar as 5.1 (the DTS core, not DTS-HD MA or DTS:X). Needs HDMI ARC/eARC with Digital Sound Out on Pass Through or Auto.

Videos only. An audio-only DTS file can come out as loud noise.
